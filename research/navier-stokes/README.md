# Navier–Stokes Equations

Reading notes and preliminary mathematical questions about Navier–Stokes equations, especially **Liouville-type theorems** and **regularity theory**.

## Reading directions

### Liouville-type problems

- Stationary and ancient solutions
- Rigidity, classification, and nonexistence
- Decay and integrability assumptions
- Scaling-critical spaces and blow-up limits

### Regularity

- Local energy inequalities and suitable weak solutions
- $\varepsilon$-regularity and partial regularity
- A priori estimates and criticality

### Other directions

- Boundary effects and half-space problems
- Connections with mathematical questions suggested by fluid-mechanics papers
